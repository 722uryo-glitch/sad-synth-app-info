SAD Synth Local Automation

A personal desktop tool for the owner of the puffer! music channel.

The tool prepares original AI-generated ambient synth music, checks media files, assembles videos and thumbnails, and uploads or schedules approved videos through the YouTube Data API. It sends operational failure notices through the owner's Gmail account to a configured recipient.

Personal use

This application is operated locally on the owner's Windows computer. It is not a public sign-in service and does not offer accounts to visitors. Google account access requires the owner's explicit authorization.

Google permissions

YouTube authorization permits upload, metadata management, channel verification, and scheduled publication. Google's permission also covers other video operations; the application's normal workflow is limited to operating the configured channel. Gmail authorization is used to send status and failure notifications, not to read the mailbox.

Contact

The operator can be contacted through the public contact information on the puffer! YouTube channel.
