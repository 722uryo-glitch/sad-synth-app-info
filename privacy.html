Privacy Policy

SAD Synth Local Automation
Effective date: October 21, 2026

Purpose and access

This personal desktop application accesses the channel identifier, video metadata, upload sessions, processing status, thumbnails, and scheduled publication settings needed to operate the owner's puffer! YouTube channel. It uses Gmail send permission to deliver operational notices to the owner's configured recipient. It does not read Gmail messages or the owner's inbox.

Storage

OAuth credentials, local upload-session records, generated media, production logs, and notification receipts are stored on the owner's computer. Credentials and upload sessions are kept in an access-restricted local directory. Local records remain until the owner removes them or revokes access. This website does not collect passwords, OAuth tokens, or uploaded media.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only to provide the channel operation and notification features described here. Necessary video files and metadata are sent to YouTube; notification contents and the configured recipient are sent to Gmail. The operator may use an automation assistant to review generated media and operational results. Credentials and mailbox contents are not supplied to music or image generation models. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and revocation

The owner can stop the local application, delete local credentials and records, and revoke Google access at Google Account permissions. Revocation prevents future authorized API use. Deleting local media does not automatically remove videos already uploaded to YouTube; those can be managed in YouTube Studio.

This website

This static website has no analytics scripts, account forms, or advertising. The hosting provider may maintain standard access logs under its own privacy policy.

Contact and updates

Contact the operator through the public contact information on the puffer! channel. Changes to the application's data practices will be reflected in this policy and, where needed, require renewed authorization.
